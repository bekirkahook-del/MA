Accessibility

Last updated November 17, 2026

We want M&A Prep to work for everyone, including people who use screen readers, keyboards or other assistive tools. We aim to follow the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.2 level AA.

What we do

  • Pages use clear headings and labels, and images have text alternatives.
  • You can reach buttons and forms with the keyboard, and the focused item is visibly outlined.
  • Buttons use plain labels, and we respect your device's "reduce motion" setting.
  • We check text and background contrast as we build.

Known gaps

Some interactive tools, charts and games may not yet work perfectly with every assistive technology. We're improving them.

Tell us

If something is hard to use, email hello@mnaprep.com with the page and what happened. We'll respond within 5 business days and work on a fix or an alternative way to get the content.
