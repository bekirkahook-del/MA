Cookie & Local Storage Policy

Last updated November 17, 2026

M&A Prep doesn't use advertising or cross-site tracking cookies. We use your browser's local storage (a small first-party store) for the things below.

WhatWhyType
Sign-in sessionKeeps you signed in (set by our login provider, Supabase)Strictly necessary
Progress and settingsSaves XP, answers, streaks and preferences on your deviceStrictly necessary
Visitor ID, visit day, time-on-section totalsAnonymous visit counts and which sections are used, so we can improve the siteAnalytics (first-party, no name or email attached)
Stripe checkoutWhen you pay, Stripe's own page may set cookies it needs to process the payment and prevent fraudStrictly necessary (third party)

Fonts are loaded from Google Fonts, so your browser contacts Google to fetch them.

Your choice

You can turn off visit counting in this browser right now. We also turn it off automatically if your browser sends Global Privacy Control or Do Not Track.

Clearing data

You can clear local storage in your browser settings at any time. That signs you out and removes progress saved only on this device.
