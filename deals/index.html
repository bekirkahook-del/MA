Common questions

What was the biggest M&A deal of the last 12 months?
In this list, the largest is SpaceX and xAI at ~$250B (2026 Q1). Combines Starlink's satellite network with xAI's AI models, both controlled by Elon Musk. Value is press-reported.
Why should students follow M&A deals?
Interviewers often ask you to walk through a recent deal: who bought whom, the price, and why it makes sense. Knowing 2–3 deals well is an easy way to stand out.
How do I talk about a deal in an interview?
Lead with the buyer, target and price, then the strategic reason, then one risk or what you'd watch. Keep it under a minute.
