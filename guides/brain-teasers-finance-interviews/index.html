Brain teasers and mental math for finance interviews

Updated Nov 3, 2026 · M&A Prep

Some banks and most trading desks throw in brain teasers or quick mental math. They're testing how you think under pressure, so talk through your steps out loud even when you know the answer.

If you get stuck, set up the problem with simple variables, try a smaller version, and sanity-check your result before you say it.

Brain teasers

Basic

What is 17 × 24? Explain how you got it.

A strong answer covers
  • Answer: 408
  • Breaks it down, e.g. 17 × 25 = 425, minus 17
  • Calm, clear mental math
  • Fast
Basic

What is 15% of 360?

A strong answer covers
  • Answer: 54
  • 10% = 36, 5% = 18, sum 54
  • Explains the shortcut
  • Fast
Basic

What's 25% of 480 plus 12% of 50?

A strong answer covers
  • 25% of 480 = 120
  • 12% of 50 = 6
  • Answer: 126
  • Clear mental steps
Intermediate

How many gas stations are there in the United States?

A strong answer covers
  • Structured approach (cars, fill-ups, station capacity or population per station)
  • States assumptions clearly
  • Lands on a reasonable number (the industry estimate is about 150,000 fueling sites)
  • Sanity-checks the result
Intermediate

A bat and a ball cost $1.10 together. The bat costs $1.00 more than the ball. How much is the ball?

A strong answer covers
  • Answer: $0.05
  • Sets up ball = x, bat = x + 1.00
  • 2x + 1.00 = 1.10
  • Avoids the intuitive $0.10 trap
Intermediate

What's the angle between the hour and minute hands at 3:15?

A strong answer covers
  • Answer: 7.5 degrees
  • Minute hand at 90°
  • Hour hand moves 0.5° per minute: 90 + 7.5 = 97.5°
  • Difference is 7.5°
Intermediate

I'll pay you $1 for each dot on a die you roll, or you can re-roll once. What's the game worth?

A strong answer covers
  • Single roll expected value is 3.5
  • Re-roll if the first roll is 3 or less
  • EV = (4+5+6)/6 + (1/2)(3.5) = 2.5 + 1.75
  • Answer: $4.25
Intermediate

How many times a day do a clock's hour and minute hands overlap?

A strong answer covers
  • Answer: 22
  • They overlap 11 times every 12 hours
  • Not 24, because they don't overlap every hour
  • Explains the logic
Advanced

You have 8 balls, one slightly heavier, and a balance scale. What's the fewest weighings to find it?

A strong answer covers
  • Answer: 2
  • Split into groups of 3, 3 and 2
  • Weigh 3 vs 3; then weigh within the heavier group or the 2
  • Explains why 1 weighing isn't enough
Advanced

Estimate the size of the U.S. college textbook market.

A strong answer covers
  • Students × courses × spending per course
  • States assumptions (about 19M students, rentals and used books)
  • Reasonable final range
  • Sanity check against total spending per student
Advanced

You flip a fair coin until you get two heads in a row. What's the expected number of flips?

A strong answer covers
  • Answer: 6
  • Sets up states: start, one head
  • E = 1 + ½E1 + ½E and E1 = 1 + ½·0 + ½E
  • Solves the equations cleanly

Quick quiz (8 questions)

Revenue grows from 80 to 100. What's the growth rate?
  1. 20%
  2. 25% ✓
  3. 80%
  4. 125%
(100 − 80) ÷ 80 = 25%.
Revenue falls from 100 to 80. What's the decline?
  1. 20% ✓
  2. 25%
  3. 80%
  4. −25%
(100 − 80) ÷ 100 = 20%.
A business doubles in 5 years. Its annual growth rate is closest to:
  1. 10%
  2. 15% ✓
  3. 20%
  4. 40%
2^(1/5) ≈ 1.149, so about 15% a year.
What's 15% of 80?
  1. 12 ✓
  2. 8
  3. 15
  4. 18
10% is 8, 5% is 4: 12.
What's 25% of 360?
  1. 80
  2. 100
  3. 90 ✓
  4. 120
360 ÷ 4 = 90.
Sales go from 50 to 60. Growth is:
  1. 20% ✓
  2. 10%
  3. 16.7%
  4. 25%
10 ÷ 50 = 20%.
Sales go from 60 back to 50. Decline is:
  1. 16.7% ✓
  2. 20%
  3. 10%
  4. 25%
10 ÷ 60 ≈ 16.7%.
Using the Rule of 72, a market growing 8% a year doubles in about:
  1. 6 years
  2. 12 years
  3. 18 years
  4. 9 years ✓
72 ÷ 8 = 9 years.
