Coffee chat questions for finance networking (and how to ask for one)

A coffee chat is a short call (usually 15 to 30 minutes) with someone who works where you want to work. In banking, these chats matter a lot. They're how you learn about a group, and how a banker might remember your name when applications come in.

The goal isn't to ask for a job. It's to learn, be easy to talk to, and leave them thinking you'd be a good fit.

How to ask for a chat

Template

A simple email or LinkedIn message

"Hi [Name], I'm a sophomore at [School] studying finance and really interested in investment banking. I saw you're in [Group] at [Bank] and would love to hear about your experience. Would you have 15 minutes for a quick call in the next couple of weeks? Thanks so much, [Your name]"

Keep it short, mention something you have in common (same school, club or hometown), and make the ask small. If they don't answer, one polite follow-up a week later is fine.

Good questions to ask

About their path

  • How did you end up in banking, and in this group?
  • What surprised you most in your first year?
  • What do you wish you'd known before you started?

About the job

  • What does a normal week look like for you?
  • What kinds of deals has your group been working on?
  • What makes someone stand out as an analyst here?

About the firm

  • How would you describe the culture compared to other banks?
  • How do analysts get staffed on deals?
  • Is there anyone else you'd suggest I talk to?

That last question is the most important one. It's how one chat turns into three.

Common questions

What should I not ask in a coffee chat?
Don't ask how much they get paid, don't ask things you could find on the bank's website, and don't ask for a referral in the first chat. Let that come naturally later.
How long should a coffee chat be?
Plan for 15 to 20 minutes and respect their time. If they want to keep talking, great, but offer to let them go when time is up.
How do I follow up after a coffee chat?
Send a thank-you note within a day that mentions one specific thing they said. Then check in every month or two with a short update, like a new internship or something you learned.
How many coffee chats should I do?
Many students who land banking jobs do dozens over a recruiting cycle. Track them in a spreadsheet or in M&A Prep's free networking tracker so you know who to follow up with.
