Equity research interview questions

Updated Nov 9, 2026 · M&A Prep

Equity research analysts study a group of companies and tell investors whether to buy, hold or sell their stocks.

Interviews focus on one thing above all: can you pitch a stock clearly and back it up?

Common questions

Pitch me a stock.
Give a buy or sell, 2–3 reasons, a price target with a simple valuation, and the main risk. About 2 minutes.
How would you value a company?
Comparable companies (multiples), precedent deals and a DCF, then compare the results.
What's the difference between equity research and investment banking?
Research analysts publish opinions on stocks for investors. Bankers advise companies on deals.
What makes a stock undervalued?
The market price is below what you think it's worth, often because investors are missing something about its growth, profits or risks.
