How to get into investment banking from Georgetown

Updated Nov 12, 2026 · M&A Prep

Georgetown has a long history of sending students to Wall Street. Finance and consulting have been the top outcomes for its graduates, and McDonough reported that 53% of its class of 2018 went into financial services.

Students build skills through investing clubs and the school's strong alumni network in New York.

Programs and clubs to know

Investing

Georgetown University Student Investment Fund (GUSIF)

  • A student-run fund where members learn to analyze financial statements, value companies and pitch investment ideas.
  • Members say it can take you from no finance background to valuing companies in a couple of semesters.

Other groups

  • The Georgetown University Alumni and Student Federal Credit Union, founded in 1983, is the oldest financial group on campus.
  • Georgetown also has an alumni Wall Street Alliance, a good way to find alumni at banks.

Your timeline at Georgetown

WhenWhat to do
Freshman fallJoin 1–2 finance clubs. Keep your GPA high (many programs want 3.5+, some 3.7+).
Freshman spring–summerLearn accounting and valuation basics. Start 1–2 alumni coffee chats a month. Apply to early-insight programs.
Sophomore fall–springApply to your school's selective IB program. Main window for junior-summer IB internships: applications, interviews and Superdays (recent cycles opened as early as sophomore fall).
Junior summerThe internship that usually turns into your full-time offer.

Recruiting runs about 1.5–2 years ahead of the internship, so the work starts earlier than most students expect. See the full recruiting timeline.

How to stand out

What actually moves the needle

  • Join GUSIF or another finance club freshman year.
  • Reach out to Georgetown alumni in New York and DC. Hoyas tend to help Hoyas.
  • Nail the technicals: the three statements, enterprise value, DCF and a paper LBO. Practice them out loud.
  • Network early with alumni from your school at banks. A warm intro often decides who gets the first interview.
  • Have a tight 60-second story for "walk me through your resume" and "why investment banking."

Common questions

Is Georgetown a target school for investment banking?
Yes. Georgetown sends many students to banks every year, and finance has long been one of its top career outcomes.
What's the best finance club at Georgetown?
GUSIF is one of the best known, but any club that teaches valuation and connects you to alumni helps.
