Goldman Sachs investment banking interview guide

Updated Nov 12, 2026 · M&A Prep

Goldman Sachs runs one of the most competitive summer analyst programs on Wall Street. The good news: the process is simple and Goldman explains it publicly. There are two main steps, a short video interview and a Superday.

Goldman says it cares more about how you think than about one "right" answer, so practice talking through your reasoning out loud.

The interview process, step by step

RoundWhat happens
ApplicationApply online. Goldman suggests looking at every division first, since the same skills fit several.
Video interviewAbout 30 minutes, recorded on your own time. Questions cover your resume, your skills and what you know about Goldman's divisions.
Superday2 to 5 interviews depending on the division, with people you could actually work with.

M&A Prep's application tracker lists the 2028 Summer Analyst as Jan 2027. Postings can open and close early, so check the bank's careers site often.

What Goldman Sachs interviewers focus on

Bulge bracket · New York

  • How you reason through a problem, not just the final answer. Goldman's former HR head said there's often "no right answer" and they watch how you work through it.
  • Knowing Goldman's divisions and why you picked yours.
  • Your experience, background and how you work with others, inside and outside of school.

Questions to practice

TypeExamples
BehavioralWalk me through your resume. Tell me about a time you worked on a team under pressure. Why Goldman?
TechnicalHow do the 3 statements link? Walk me through a DCF. Why might two companies with the same EBITDA have different EVs?
ThinkingOpen-ended problems where you explain your approach step by step.

How to prepare for Goldman Sachs

Do these before your first round

  • Use Goldman's own events (My GS Events) and campus ambassadors to learn the divisions before you apply.
  • Know the core technicals cold: how the 3 statements link, enterprise value vs. equity value, a DCF walkthrough and a paper LBO. See the IB interview questions guide.
  • Have a 60-second "walk me through your resume" and a specific why investment banking answer.
  • Prepare a real "why Goldman Sachs" answer: a recent deal, a group you want, and a person you spoke with.
  • Follow the markets for 2 weeks before interviews and have one stock pitch ready (how to pitch a stock).

Common questions

How many interviews are in a Goldman Superday?
Goldman says it typically runs 2 to 5 interviews for campus hires, depending on the division.
Does Goldman use HireVue?
Goldman's first round is a recorded video interview of about 30 minutes that you complete on your own schedule.
Is the video interview technical?
It's mostly about your resume, skills and interest in Goldman's divisions. Expect technicals at the Superday.
