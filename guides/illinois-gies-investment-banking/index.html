How to get into investment banking from Illinois

Updated Nov 10, 2026 · M&A Prep

At Illinois, the Gies Investment Banking Academy (part of the Stanley C. Golder Academies) is the most structured path into investment banking.

It's open to sophomores and juniors and is highly selective, so freshman year is about building your grades and basics.

The Gies Investment Banking Academy

Sophomores &amp; juniors

What it is

  • A highly selective program for current sophomores and juniors, aimed at full-time roles at top banks.
  • Upperclass members mentor newly selected sophomores.
  • Technical training, case competitions, interview and internship prep, and alumni networking.
  • Hands-on practice with banking software in the Margolis Market Information Lab.

Your timeline at Illinois

WhenWhat to do
Freshman fallJoin 1–2 finance clubs. Keep your GPA high (many programs want 3.5+, some 3.7+).
Freshman spring–summerLearn accounting and valuation basics. Start 1–2 alumni coffee chats a month. Apply to early-insight programs.
Sophomore fall–springApply to your school's selective IB program. Main window for junior-summer IB internships: applications, interviews and Superdays (recent cycles opened as early as sophomore fall).
Junior summerThe internship that usually turns into your full-time offer.

Recruiting runs about 1.5–2 years ahead of the internship, so the work starts earlier than most students expect. See the full recruiting timeline.

How to stand out

What actually moves the needle

  • Chicago banks and boutiques are close by. Network with Illinois alumni there.
  • Get interview-ready technicals before you apply so the Academy sees you as a strong candidate.
  • Nail the technicals: the three statements, enterprise value, DCF and a paper LBO. Practice them out loud.
  • Network early with alumni from your school at banks. A warm intro often decides who gets the first interview.
  • Have a tight 60-second story for "walk me through your resume" and "why investment banking."

Common questions

Who can apply to the Gies Investment Banking Academy?
Current sophomores and juniors. Gies describes admission as highly selective.
Is Illinois good for investment banking?
Illinois students recruit for banking every year, and the Golder Academies give structured training. Your prep and networking still matter most.
