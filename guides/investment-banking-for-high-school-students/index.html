How to prepare for investment banking in high school

Updated Nov 5, 2026 · M&A Prep

You can't work at a bank in high school, but you can get a real head start. Most students don't learn what banking is until college, so knowing early is an advantage.

Focus on three things: grades, getting into a strong college, and learning the basics.

What to do now

Keep your grades high

Strong grades open doors to colleges where banks recruit.

Choose colleges carefully

Look at which schools banks recruit from and how strong their finance clubs and alumni networks are.

Learn the basics

What stocks, companies and the three financial statements are. Short daily practice adds up.

Start something

An investing club, a stock competition, a small business, or a part-time job. All of it shows initiative.

Common questions

What should I major in to become an investment banker?
Finance, economics and accounting are common, but banks hire many majors. Pick something you can do well in.
Is it too early to learn finance in high school?
No. Students who already know the basics walk into college finance clubs ahead of everyone else.
