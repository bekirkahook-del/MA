The investment banking recruiting timeline, year by year

Updated Nov 3, 2026 · M&A Prep

At large banks, most full-time analyst offers come from junior-summer internships, and recruiting for those internships often starts during sophomore year. That means preparation really starts freshman year.

The usual path: build your GPA and join an investment club as a freshman, network and learn technicals so you're interview-ready as a sophomore, intern the summer after junior year, then convert that internship into a full-time offer. If you miss the main cycle, middle-market banks, boutiques and off-cycle roles often hire later.

Year-by-year timeline

WhenWhat to focus on
Freshman yearGPA, join an investment club, first coffee chats with alumni, learn the basics
Sophomore yearTechnicals and your story ready; networking in earnest; many banks start junior-summer recruiting; early insight programs
Junior yearInterviews wrap up for many banks; prepare for the internship
Summer after junior yearThe internship: about 10 weeks that work as a long interview
Senior yearReturn offer, or recruit for full-time roles at banks still hiring
After graduationStart as a full-time analyst, usually in the summer

Timing varies by bank and year, so check each firm's application dates.
