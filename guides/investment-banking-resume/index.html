How to write an investment banking resume

Updated Nov 5, 2026 · M&A Prep

Bankers spend under a minute on each resume. Yours needs to be one clean page that shows good grades, real experience and interest in finance, fast.

Fancy design doesn't help. Clear, specific bullets do.

The standard format

Education (top)

  • School, major, expected graduation
  • GPA if it's strong
  • Relevant classes or honors

Experience

  • Most recent first
  • 3–4 bullets per role
  • Start each bullet with an action verb

Leadership and activities

  • Finance clubs, student investment funds, sports, jobs
  • Show responsibility, not just membership

Skills and interests

  • Excel, PowerPoint, languages
  • 1–2 real interests (they start conversations)

Weak vs. strong bullets

WeakStrong
Helped with stock researchPitched 2 stocks to a 40-member club; one was added to the $25K portfolio
Worked at a restaurantTrained 6 new hires and handled about $3K in nightly sales

Common questions

Should my investment banking resume be one page?
Yes. Always one page for students and analysts.
What if I have no finance experience?
Use what you have. Any job, club or project can show work ethic, numbers and leadership. Add a finance club or a stock pitch to show interest.
Should I put my GPA on my resume?
Usually yes. Leaving it off makes recruiters assume it's low.
