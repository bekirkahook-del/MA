IRR and MOIC cheat sheet for interviews

Updated Nov 3, 2026 · M&A Prep

MOIC (multiple on invested capital) is simple: money out ÷ money in. IRR is the annual return that accounts for time: the same 2.0x is great over 3 years (about 26%) and mediocre over 7 (about 10%).

In interviews you won't have a calculator, so learn a few anchors. The table below is computed exactly as MOIC^(1/years) − 1, rounded to the nearest percent. The Rule of 72 helps too: money doubles in about 72 ÷ rate years.
