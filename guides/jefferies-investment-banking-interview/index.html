Jefferies investment banking interview guide

Updated Nov 10, 2026 · M&A Prep

Jefferies is a full-service investment bank headquartered in New York with 30+ offices. It's known for strong deal flow and giving junior bankers hands-on work.

Its process can include online tests, a phone screen and a Superday with a case you present.

The interview process, step by step

RoundWhat happens
ApplicationOnline, through campus recruiting or an employee referral.
Online testsNumerical reasoning (reading financial documents) and verbal reasoning.
Phone screen + first roundAbout 30 minutes, then a mostly behavioral interview. Technicals are possible.
Superday3 one-on-one interviews plus a case study you prepare in about an hour and present.

M&A Prep's application tracker lists the 2028 Summer Analyst as Dec 2026. Postings can open and close early, so check the bank's careers site often.

What Jefferies interviewers focus on

Global investment bank · New York

  • Cultural fit and personal drive.
  • Presenting a case clearly when everyone gets a similar case.
  • Knowing a recent Jefferies deal.

Questions to practice

TypeExamples
FitWhy Jefferies? Walk me through your resume. Where else are you interviewing?
TechnicalValuation methods and their pros and cons. Walk through a DCF or LBO. How depreciation flows through the statements.
CaseAnalyze a company or deal in about an hour and present your view.

How to prepare for Jefferies

Do these before your first round

  • Practice a 10-minute case presentation with a timer.
  • Know the core technicals cold: how the 3 statements link, enterprise value vs. equity value, a DCF walkthrough and a paper LBO. See the IB interview questions guide.
  • Have a 60-second "walk me through your resume" and a specific why investment banking answer.
  • Prepare a real "why Jefferies" answer: a recent deal, a group you want, and a person you spoke with.
  • Follow the markets for 2 weeks before interviews and have one stock pitch ready (how to pitch a stock).

Common questions

Does Jefferies have a case study?
The guide we read says the Superday includes a case you prepare for about an hour and present.
How long does the Jefferies process take?
Typically 1 to 2 months from application to offer.
Are there online tests?
Yes, numerical and verbal reasoning tests can come early.
