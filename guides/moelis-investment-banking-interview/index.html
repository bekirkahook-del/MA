Moelis investment banking interview guide

Updated Nov 17, 2026 · M&A Prep

Moelis publishes its recruiting steps, which makes it one of the easier firms to plan for. There is an online application with a short assessment, a first round with bankers, and an onsite final round.

Moelis also runs a Leadership Development Program for sophomores that comes with a chance to interview for the summer analyst program.

The interview process, step by step

RoundWhat happens
ApplicationSubmit your resume on Moelis's student application site. US and UK applicants also complete the Suited Assessment, which takes about 30 minutes and measures behavioral traits and cognitive skills.
First roundOne or two interviews with investment bankers, covering technical and behavioral questions.
Second roundAn onsite final round with several senior bankers.

M&A Prep's application tracker lists the 2028 Summer Analyst (Houston) as Opened Oct 5. Postings can open and close early, so check the bank's careers site often.

What Moelis interviewers focus on

Elite boutique · New York

  • Technical basics: financial statements and valuation methods, which Moelis tells candidates to review.
  • Knowing Moelis: its vision, values, recent news and transactions.
  • Curiosity, drive and enthusiasm. Moelis says it values these and tells candidates to be authentic.

Questions to practice

TypeExamples

DealsTell me about a recent Moelis transaction and what you found interesting about it.

How to prepare for Moelis

Do these before your first round

  • Do the Suited Assessment early and in one sitting. It's part of the application, not an extra.
  • Use Moelis's own advice: review financial statements and valuation methods, and do mock interviews.
  • Know the core technicals cold: how the 3 statements link, enterprise value vs. equity value, a DCF walkthrough and a paper LBO. See the IB interview questions guide.
  • Have a 60-second "walk me through your resume" and a specific why investment banking answer.
  • Prepare a real "why Moelis" answer: a recent deal, a group you want, and a person you spoke with.
  • Follow the markets for 2 weeks before interviews and have one stock pitch ready (how to pitch a stock).

Common questions

When do Moelis summer analyst applications open?
Moelis says US applications typically open in December of your sophomore year. London and Beijing usually open in August, and Hong Kong in May, for internships starting the next year.
How long is the Moelis summer analyst program?
Moelis lists its US, London, Beijing and Hong Kong summer analyst programs as 10-week internships for undergraduates.
What is the Moelis Leadership Development Program?
It is a program for select, high-achieving sophomores interested in finance. It includes industry speakers, time with Moelis employees and deal case studies, and an opportunity to interview for the US Summer Analyst program.
