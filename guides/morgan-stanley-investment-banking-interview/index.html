Morgan Stanley investment banking interview guide

Updated Nov 11, 2026 · M&A Prep

Morgan Stanley's investment banking summer program is non-rotational: you're placed in one coverage or product group (like TMT, healthcare, sponsors or M&A) for the summer. On the application you rank offices and groups, so know what you want.

Expect a HireVue, a short first round for some candidates, then a Superday where fit and motivation matter a lot.

The interview process, step by step

RoundWhat happens
ApplicationRolling review. You rank up to 3 offices and 3 groups.
HireVue3 to 5 recorded questions, due within a couple of days.
First round20–30 minute call with an analyst or associate. Some New York candidates go straight to the Superday.
SuperdayAbout 3 back-to-back interviews mixing technicals and motivation.

M&A Prep's application tracker lists the 2028 Summer Analyst as Jan 2027. Postings can open and close early, so check the bank's careers site often.

What Morgan Stanley interviewers focus on

Bulge bracket · New York

  • Fit and motivation: why banking, why Morgan Stanley and why your group.
  • Behavioral answers that line up with Morgan Stanley's published core values (doing the right thing, putting clients first, leading with exceptional ideas, diversity and inclusion, giving back).
  • Technical follow-ups that go past the first textbook answer.

Questions to practice

TypeExamples
TechnicalWalk me through a DCF. When doesn't Gordon Growth work? How do the statements link? Accretion/dilution basics. FIFO vs. LIFO.
MarketsWhere is the S&P 500 going in 12 months? Pitch a company you like.
BehavioralTell me about yourself. A challenge you overcame. A time you did the right thing.

How to prepare for Morgan Stanley

Do these before your first round

  • Read Morgan Stanley's core values and prepare one story for each.
  • Know the core technicals cold: how the 3 statements link, enterprise value vs. equity value, a DCF walkthrough and a paper LBO. See the IB interview questions guide.
  • Have a 60-second "walk me through your resume" and a specific why investment banking answer.
  • Prepare a real "why Morgan Stanley" answer: a recent deal, a group you want, and a person you spoke with.
  • Follow the markets for 2 weeks before interviews and have one stock pitch ready (how to pitch a stock).

Common questions

Is the Morgan Stanley summer program rotational?
No. You're placed in one group for the summer.
Does Morgan Stanley use HireVue?
Yes, the guide we read describes a recorded HireVue with 3 to 5 questions.
What do they care about most?
Fit and motivation weigh heavily, along with solid technicals.
