How to get into investment banking from Rutgers

Updated Nov 12, 2026 · M&A Prep

Rutgers Business School is about an hour from New York, and it runs programs to connect students with Wall Street.

Join a finance club early and apply to the school's hands-on programs.

Programs and clubs to know

Start here

Road to Wall Street and LIBOR

  • Road to Wall Street is a Rutgers Business School program that links students to Wall Street careers.
  • Little Investment Bankers of Rutgers (LIBOR), a student group, ran its 12th Annual Finance Summit in New York in 2019 with over 300 students, alumni and faculty.

Scarlet Student Managed Fund

  • A $1 million fund from a 2025 alumni gift, per Rutgers Business School.
  • Its first class of 16 included undergraduate honors finance students alongside MBA and graduate students.
  • A two-semester course with stock pitching, research and mentoring.

Your timeline at Rutgers

WhenWhat to do
Freshman fallJoin 1–2 finance clubs. Keep your GPA high (many programs want 3.5+, some 3.7+).
Freshman spring–summerLearn accounting and valuation basics. Start 1–2 alumni coffee chats a month. Apply to early-insight programs.
Sophomore fall–springApply to your school's selective IB program. Main window for junior-summer IB internships: applications, interviews and Superdays (recent cycles opened as early as sophomore fall).
Junior summerThe internship that usually turns into your full-time offer.

Recruiting runs about 1.5–2 years ahead of the internship, so the work starts earlier than most students expect. See the full recruiting timeline.

How to stand out

What actually moves the needle

  • Join LIBOR and go to its events to meet alumni on Wall Street.
  • Take the train to New York for coffee chats. Rutgers is close enough for regular trips.
  • Nail the technicals: the three statements, enterprise value, DCF and a paper LBO. Practice them out loud.
  • Network early with alumni from your school at banks. A warm intro often decides who gets the first interview.
  • Have a tight 60-second story for "walk me through your resume" and "why investment banking."

Common questions

Does Rutgers have an investment banking club?
Yes. Little Investment Bankers of Rutgers (LIBOR) is a student group that has run an annual finance summit in New York.
Can Rutgers undergrads join the Scarlet Fund?
Yes. Select undergraduate honors finance students join MBA and graduate students in the $1 million fund.
