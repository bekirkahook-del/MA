How to get into sales and trading from TCU

Updated Nov 16, 2026 · M&A Prep

Sales and trading (S&T) teams buy and sell stocks, bonds and other assets for big clients. Salespeople talk to clients and bring in orders; traders execute the trades and manage the bank's risk.

Interviews test whether you follow markets and think fast with numbers. The clubs and programs below come from our TCU guide; the interview topics come from our S&T guide.

What S&T interviews test

What to expect

  • Market awareness: "Where are the markets today?" and one reason for a recent move.
  • Pitching a trade: what to buy or sell, the catalyst, the risks and your exit, in about 2 minutes.
  • Fast mental math and brain teasers.
  • Basics like what happens to bond prices when interest rates go up (they go down) and the difference between sales and trading.

Common questions

How do I prepare for sales and trading from TCU?
Follow markets every day, practice mental math and prepare a 2-minute trade pitch. Use the clubs and programs above to practice with other students.
What's the difference between sales and trading?
Salespeople talk to clients and bring in orders. Traders execute the trades and manage the bank's risk.
What happens to bond prices when interest rates go up?
Bond prices go down. New bonds pay more, so older bonds with lower rates are worth less.
