How to get into hedge funds from Texas Tech

Updated Nov 16, 2026 · M&A Prep

A hedge fund pools money from wealthy people and institutions and tries to earn strong returns in any market, up or down. Common ways in are investment banking, equity research or trading, and being able to pitch a stock well is key.

The clubs and programs below come from our Texas Tech guide; the pitch structure and interview topics come from our hedge fund guides.

The path from Texas Tech

WhenWhat to do

Clubs and programs to know at Texas Tech

These come from our Texas Tech investment banking guide, where each one is described and sourced.

Real money

Student Managed Investment Fund (SMIF)

  • Started in 1997 with endowment gifts that totaled $750,000.
  • Students work in teams in a classroom with 10 Bloomberg terminals and become Bloomberg certified.
  • Many SMIF students go into security analysis and investment management.

Finance Association

  • A student group for finance majors.
  • In a 2019 trip, 15 finance students visited Dallas firms like Citi, Comerica Bank and Charles Schwab.

What hedge fund interviews test

What to expect

  • Your stock pitch: the call (long or short with a price target), two or three thesis points, why the market is wrong (your variant view), a catalyst and valuation.
  • Risk follow-ups: what would make you wrong, how you'd size the position, and what you'd do if it fell 20%.
  • Markets knowledge: current levels and what's driving them, plus how rate changes affect stocks and bonds.
  • Basics like stock vs. bond, long vs. short and the main strategies (long/short equity, global macro, event-driven, quant).

Common questions

How do I get a hedge fund job from Texas Tech?
Common paths are investment banking, equity research or trading, and a strong stock pitch is key. Start with the clubs and programs above, then practice pitching out loud.
How do hedge funds make money?
They charge a management fee on the money they run and keep a share of the profits they make for investors.
What's the difference between a hedge fund and a mutual fund?
Mutual funds are open to everyone and mostly buy stocks and bonds. Hedge funds are for wealthy investors and use more strategies, like shorting and leverage.
