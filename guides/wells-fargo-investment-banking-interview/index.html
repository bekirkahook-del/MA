Wells Fargo investment banking interview guide

Updated Nov 12, 2026 · M&A Prep

Wells Fargo's corporate and investment bank hires summer analysts mainly in New York and Charlotte. Its interviews are more structured than most: each round asks for specific examples graded on the same criteria.

Expect a HireVue, sometimes a first round, then a 3-interview Superday.

The interview process, step by step

RoundWhat happens
ApplicationRolling, by division and city. Applications open more than a year before the internship.
HireVueAbout 4 to 6 recorded behavioral and motivation questions.
First round (not everyone)30-minute video call with 2 bankers: resume, why Wells Fargo, conceptual technicals.
Superday3 back-to-back 30-minute interviews, usually 2 technical and 1 behavioral.

M&A Prep's application tracker lists the 2028 Summer Internship as Jan 2027. Postings can open and close early, so check the bank's careers site often.

What Wells Fargo interviewers focus on

Bulge bracket · New York & Charlotte

  • Specific examples for every behavioral question (competency-based grading).
  • Explaining technicals out loud without a model.
  • Ethics, customer focus and leadership stories.

Questions to practice

TypeExamples
BehavioralTight deadline with little information. A mistake you caught. Taking initiative. Reaching a compromise.
TechnicalHow a debt issuance flows through the statements. Levered vs. unlevered cash flow. Net debt from EV and equity value. LBO returns.
MacroTariffs, the yield curve, how the Fed affects long-term rates.

How to prepare for Wells Fargo

Do these before your first round

  • Write out 8 STAR stories (situation, task, action, result) before your HireVue.
  • Know the core technicals cold: how the 3 statements link, enterprise value vs. equity value, a DCF walkthrough and a paper LBO. See the IB interview questions guide.
  • Have a 60-second "walk me through your resume" and a specific why investment banking answer.
  • Prepare a real "why Wells Fargo" answer: a recent deal, a group you want, and a person you spoke with.
  • Follow the markets for 2 weeks before interviews and have one stock pitch ready (how to pitch a stock).
