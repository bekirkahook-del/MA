What do investment banking analysts do? A day in the life

Updated Nov 5, 2026 · M&A Prep

Analysts are the entry-level bankers, usually hired right out of college for a 2- to 3-year program.

They do much of the detailed work behind every deal: the numbers, the slides and the research.

The main tasks

Financial models

Excel models that value companies and test deal scenarios.

Pitch books

PowerPoint decks used to win clients and present ideas.

Deal work

Organizing documents, answering buyer questions and tracking every step of a live deal.

Research

Company and industry research, comparable companies and past deals.

A typical day

TimeWhat happens
MorningCheck emails, update models with new numbers
AfternoonTeam calls, edit the pitch deck, research comps
EveningTurn comments from senior bankers into new drafts
Late nightFinal checks before materials go to the client

Common questions

What skills do investment banking analysts need?
Strong Excel and PowerPoint, attention to detail, and the ability to work fast under pressure.
What happens after the analyst program?
Analysts get promoted to associate, or leave for private equity, hedge funds, corporate jobs or startups.
