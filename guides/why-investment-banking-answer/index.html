How to answer "Why investment banking?" in an interview

Updated Nov 5, 2026 · M&A Prep

"Why investment banking?" comes up in almost every banking interview, and a weak answer can sink an otherwise strong candidate. Interviewers want to know you understand the job, that you've thought about it, and that you'll stick it out through the long hours.

The best answers are short, specific and backed by things you've actually done.

A simple 3-part structure

Part 1

1. Where the interest started

One real moment: a class, a club, an internship, a deal you followed, a family business sale.

Part 2

2. What you've done about it

Proof you went deeper: finance club, a stock pitch, networking with bankers, an internship, learning the technicals.

Part 3

3. Why banking fits what you want

2 reasons tied to the job itself: the learning curve, working on live deals, the team, seeing how companies make big decisions.

Sample answer (about 60 seconds)

"I first got into finance when my uncle sold his manufacturing business and I saw how much the advisors shaped the outcome. That made me want to understand how deals actually work, so I joined our investment club, pitched two stocks, and started talking to bankers. Every conversation pointed to the same thing: banking is the fastest way to learn how companies are valued and how deals come together, and you're doing real work on live transactions from day one. I want that learning curve, and I like working on a small team under pressure, which is how I spent most of my time on our club's pitch competition."

Swap in your own story. The structure is what matters.

Mistakes to avoid

Should I say I want to do investment banking for the money?
Don't lead with it. Everyone knows the pay is good. Saying it first makes it sound like you'll leave the moment something easier comes along.
Is it okay to say I want to go to private equity after banking?
Be careful. It's common and interviewers know it, but making it your main reason suggests you see banking as a stepping stone. Focus on why you want banking itself.
How long should my answer to 'Why investment banking?' be?
About 45 to 90 seconds. Long enough to tell a real story, short enough that they can ask follow-ups.
What if I don't have a finance background?
That's fine. Focus on what sparked the interest and what you've done since to learn, like clubs, self-study and networking. Effort is the proof.
