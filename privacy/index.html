Privacy Policy

Last updated November 17, 2026

M&A Prep (mnaprep.com) is a free training site for students preparing for finance careers, run by Bekir Kahook in Ohio, USA. This page explains what we collect, why, and the choices you have.

What we collect

  • Account details: your email address and the name you choose, if you make an account. We collect only what we need to run the site.
  • Your progress: XP, levels, answers, streaks, profile details you add (school, graduation year, recruiting goals) and settings.
  • Resume text: if you use the resume tool, the text you upload or paste. It is used to build your profile and, if you ask for AI feedback, is sent to our AI provider.
  • Usage data: pages visited, active time, device type, approximate region and how you found us (for example a campaign link). We use a random visitor ID, not your name, and we don't use advertising or cross-site trackers.
  • Payments: if you buy Pro, payment is handled by Stripe. We never see or store your full card number. We keep your plan, status and renewal date.
  • Messages: feedback, question reports, reviews and emails you send us.

How we use it

  • To run the site, save your progress and show leaderboards.
  • To give you the Pro features you paid for and to handle billing and support.
  • To grade answers and give feedback with AI. The text you submit is sent to our AI provider to produce a response. Don't submit anything you don't want processed this way.
  • To send emails you signed up for. Every email has an unsubscribe link.
  • To understand what's working and to prevent abuse and fraud.

Cookies and local storage

We don't use advertising cookies. We store a small amount of data in your browser (local storage) to keep you signed in, save progress and count visits. Details and an opt-out are on our Cookies page. We respect the Global Privacy Control and Do Not Track browser signals by turning off visit counting.

What other people can see

Your display name, rank, XP, school and profile details you choose to add can appear on leaderboards and your public profile. Reviews you write show your display name. You can hide your school in Settings.

Who we share it with

We don't sell your personal information and we don't share it for advertising. These providers process data only to provide their service to us: Supabase (accounts and database), Netlify (hosting), Stripe (payments), Resend (email), Anthropic (AI features) and Google Fonts (fonts, which means your browser requests font files from Google). We may also disclose information if the law requires it or to protect the site and its users.

How long we keep it

We keep your account data while your account is open. When you ask us to delete your account we delete your profile, progress and email-list entry, except records we must keep for tax, fraud-prevention or legal reasons (such as payment records held by Stripe).

Your rights and choices

  • Edit your profile any time in Settings, and unsubscribe from emails with one click.
  • Ask us for a copy of your data, a correction, or deletion of your account and data by emailing hello@mnaprep.com. We'll reply within 30 days.
  • US state residents (including California, Colorado, Connecticut, Virginia and others) can ask to know, correct or delete their data and can opt out of sale or sharing. We don't sell or share personal information for advertising. We won't treat you differently for using these rights.
  • If you're in the EU, UK or similar regions: we process data to run the service you asked for (contract), for our legitimate interests in running and securing the site, and with your consent where we ask for it. You can object, withdraw consent, or complain to your local data protection authority. Data is processed in the United States.

Security

We use trusted providers, encrypted connections and access controls. No system is perfectly secure, so please use a strong, unique password.

Kids

M&A Prep is meant for college students and people preparing for college. It isn't directed at children under 13, and you must be 13 or older to make an account. If you think a child under 13 gave us information, email us and we'll delete it.

Changes

If we make a material change to this policy, we'll update the date at the top and, where we can, tell account holders by email or on the site.

Questions? Email hello@mnaprep.com.
