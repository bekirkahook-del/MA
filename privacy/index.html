Privacy Policy

Last updated November 12, 2026

M&A Prep (mnaprep.com) is a free training site for students preparing for finance careers. This page explains what we collect and why.

What we collect

  • Account details: your email address and the name you choose, if you make an account.
  • Your progress: XP, levels, answers, streaks, profile details you add (school, graduation year, recruiting goals) and settings.
  • Usage data: pages visited, time on site, device type, approximate region and how you found us. We use a random visitor ID, not your name.
  • Payments: if you buy Pro, payment is handled by Stripe. We never see or store your full card number. We keep your plan, status and renewal date.
  • Messages: feedback, question reports and emails you send us.

How we use it

  • To run the site, save your progress and show leaderboards.
  • To give you Pro features you paid for.
  • To grade answers and give feedback with AI. The text you submit is sent to our AI provider to produce a response.
  • To send emails you signed up for. Every email has an unsubscribe link.
  • To understand what's working and improve the site.

What other people can see

Your display name, rank, XP, school and profile details you choose to add can appear on leaderboards and your public profile. You can hide your school in Settings.

Who we share it with

We don't sell your data. We use these providers to run the site: Supabase (accounts and database), Netlify (hosting), Stripe (payments), Resend (email) and Anthropic (AI features). They only process data to provide their service to us.

Your choices

  • Edit your profile any time in Settings.
  • Unsubscribe from emails with one click.
  • Ask us to delete your account and data by emailing hello@mnaprep.com.

Kids

M&A Prep is meant for college students and people preparing for college. It isn't directed at children under 13.

Changes

If we change this policy, we'll update the date at the top.

Questions? Email hello@mnaprep.com.
