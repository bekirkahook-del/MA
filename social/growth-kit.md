I'm 19 and I built a free site that trains you for Wall Street interviews like a video game.

When I started looking into investment banking at Ohio State, I found out recruiting starts sophomore year, and the good prep costs hundreds of dollars.

So I built mnaprep.com:
→ 1,000+ real interview questions in 5-minute levels
→ AI mock interviews that follow up on what you actually say
→ Breakdowns of recent big deals, with quiz questions

It's free. If you're recruiting (or remember what it was like), I'd love your feedback.

**Post 2: the timeline shock**
Most freshmen don't know this: investment banking internships for the summer after junior year are often recruited during sophomore year, about 1.5–2 years early.

I built a free calculator that shows your exact dates by graduation year:
mnaprep.com/tools/recruiting-timeline-calculator/

Share it with a freshman who wants to go into finance.

**Post 3: the quiz**
Quick banking interview question:

Depreciation goes up by $10. Tax rate is 25%. What happens to cash?

Most people say it goes down. It actually goes up $2.50 (net income falls $7.50, but you add back the $10 non-cash expense).

I made a free 12-question Superday readiness test. What's your score?
mnaprep.com/tools/superday-readiness-test/

**Post 4: lessons from building**
3 things I learned building a finance training site as a freshman:
1. Students don't need more content. They need to know what to learn first.
2. Practicing out loud beats reading. Every time.
3. Recruiting rewards whoever starts earliest, not whoever's smartest.

Everything I've built is free at mnaprep.com. What would you add?

## Long description (~120 words)
M&A Prep is a free way to get ready for investment banking, private equity, hedge fund, restructuring and consulting interviews. Instead of reading 400-page guides, you clear 5-minute levels: the three statements, valuation, DCFs, LBOs, M&A and behavioral questions, with XP, streaks and titles from Intern to Managing Director. It also tracks live M&A deals, internship application deadlines and a daily market briefing, so you can talk about the market in interviews. Free tools include a Superday readiness test, a recruiting timeline calculator, IB pay by level, and interview guides for top banks and schools. I built it as a finance student because the prep I wanted didn't exist for free.
