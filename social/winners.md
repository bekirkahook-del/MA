## Patterns that win (apply to every post)
- A **name the viewer identifies with** (their school, a famous company) + a **surprising number** + a **comment fight** ("underrated or overrated?").
- **Conflict-first hook** ("Michigan isn't even top 3", "Stanford isn't top 4") beats a neutral title.
- **Speed:** same-day reaction to real news outperforms evergreen.
