The three drivers of LBO returns

EBITDA growth (the business earns more), multiple expansion (someone pays a higher multiple when you sell) and debt paydown (cash flow shrinks the debt, so more of the exit value belongs to equity). Interviewers often ask which one matters most in a given deal. Try setting the exit multiple equal to the entry multiple to see returns without multiple expansion.

Common questions

What's a good LBO return?
Private equity firms often target an IRR around 20% or more and roughly 2–3x their money over 5 years, but targets vary by firm, deal size and market.
Why does more debt raise returns?
Debt means the firm puts in less equity. If the business grows, the whole gain goes to that smaller equity check, so the percentage return is higher. The trade-off is more risk if things go wrong.
What does this calculator leave out?
Fees, interest rate details, taxes on the exit, dividends and management equity. It's built for learning and quick checks, not for a real deal model.
