Shortcuts that make this easy

  • Percentages: 15% = 10% + half of 10%. 15% of 360 = 36 + 18 = 54.
  • Flip it: x% of y = y% of x. 4% of 75 = 75% of 4 = 3.
  • Round and fix: 17 × 24 = 17 × 25 − 17 = 425 − 17 = 408.
  • Growth: (new − old) ÷ old. $80M → $100M is 20 ÷ 80 = 25%.

Common questions

What kind of math comes up in finance interviews?
Mostly quick multiplication, percentages, growth rates and rough division, for example 15% of 360 or how much revenue grew from $80M to $100M. Sales and trading interviews push speed the hardest.
How do I get faster?
Do a short drill every day, learn shortcuts (like 15% = 10% + half of 10%) and say your steps out loud. See the mental math guide.
Is this like a trading firm math test?
It's similar in spirit (timed, no calculator) but simpler. Some trading firms use longer 80-question tests in 8 minutes.
