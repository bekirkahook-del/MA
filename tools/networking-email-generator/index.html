Tips that get replies

  • Email alumni first. Shared schools get the highest reply rates.
  • Send Tuesday–Thursday mornings.
  • Ask for 15 minutes, not a job or a referral.
  • Keep a simple tracker: name, firm, date sent, follow-up date, notes.

Common questions

How long should a networking email be?
Short: 4–6 sentences. Who you are, how you found them, one specific reason you're reaching out, and a small ask (15 minutes).
What if they don't reply?
Send one polite follow-up after about a week. If there's still no reply, move on. Lots of people are just busy.
