Common questions

What is a Superday?
The final round of investment banking recruiting: several back-to-back interviews in one day, usually 3–6, with bankers from analyst to managing director. See the full Superday guide.
What questions come up in a Superday?
A mix of technicals (accounting, enterprise value, valuation, DCF, M&A and LBOs), fit questions (your story, why banking, why this firm) and sometimes quick math or a deal discussion.
How do I get better fast?
Practice out loud every day for a couple of weeks, focusing on the topics you miss. Explaining clearly matters as much as knowing the answer.
