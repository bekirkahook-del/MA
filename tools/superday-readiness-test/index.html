What a Superday is

A Superday is the final round of investment banking interviews. You usually have several back-to-back interviews in one day with analysts, associates, VPs and sometimes managing directors. Offers often come within days.

What they ask

  • Fit and behavioral: walk me through your resume, why banking, why this bank, tell me about a time you worked on a team.
  • Accounting: how the three statements link, and what happens when depreciation or working capital changes.
  • Valuation: enterprise value vs. equity value, comparable companies, precedent transactions and the DCF.
  • LBOs and M&A: why private equity uses debt, what drives returns, and accretion/dilution basics.
  • Markets and deals: a recent deal you followed and what you think of it.

How to use this test

Take it once cold, read every explanation, then take it again a few days later. If you score under 9 of 12, spend your time on the topics you missed before you spend it on rare edge cases. Interviewers care much more about clean, confident answers to the core questions than about obscure ones.

Common questions

What is a Superday?
The final round of investment banking recruiting: several back-to-back interviews in one day, usually 3–6, with bankers from analyst to managing director. See the full Superday guide.
What questions come up in a Superday?
A mix of technicals (accounting, enterprise value, valuation, DCF, M&A and LBOs), fit questions (your story, why banking, why this firm) and sometimes quick math or a deal discussion.
How do I get better fast?
Practice out loud every day for a couple of weeks, focusing on the topics you miss. Explaining clearly matters as much as knowing the answer.
